🇺🇸 What does an AI model learn from? 🎯
Loss function = the gap between prediction and answer as one number — how far the dart lands from the bullseye.
👉 Regression: MSE, classification: cross-entropy! Training loss ↓ but validation loss ↑ = overfitting.

👉 Регресст MSE, ангилалд кросс энтропи! Сургалтын loss ↓, баталгаажуулалтын loss ↑ бол overfitting.
